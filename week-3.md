

I think an important lesson for the future generations and whoever needs this lesson to learn is that Life does not always go as planned. 
Take me at 18, I had my whole life planned out which those who are older reading this will realize how silly that is. My plan was as follows a two year mission school and marriage within 4 years
of getting back then a career and children within 10. Granted this is not what happened. Within the first month of the mission I came down with a serve case of Mono. For those of you with more knowledge of mono
I came down with both types and was down and out. Swollen spleen and everything. I could not serve as it was. Within two months I had been sent home and spent the next 2ish years sleeping. 
I realistically only had a few hours of being somewhat coherent. It was that intense. Granted you know I was like alright so the mission part didn't work out as forseen but perhaps I could get
back on track during college. Haha. Suffice to say my health took a hit and then Covid hit and I came down with persistent migraines and brain fog.  Now looking from the outside in you might say 
to yourself that's rough buddy, and it was but there was a multitude of blessings and through it I have been able rely more on more on the Lord. Trusting that His plan will work out even if mine does not.

It was a transforming and refining experience. I would not nearly have as strong as testimony if I had not gone through the experiences's I had. Now you may ask what blessings? I would say 
my most treasured blessing from the experience is the ability to take a step back and look and the grander picture of God's plan and not my plan. Now to some that may seem like a bad trade, but 
honestly is one of the greatest blessings in my life. Through it all i've been able to bless others with my advice and by being able to understand and empathize when plans go off rails. 
All my experiences's are what makes me able to be a stronger disciple of Christ. It's just like training an AI. It takes many iterations and lots of adjustments but eventually you will 
get where you wanted to be. Even if the path looks different from what you imagined.
